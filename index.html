Key Findings

  • 📌 Technology is the most borrowed category, accounting for 25% of all borrowings — reflecting strong student interest in programming and data science.
  • 📈 January had the highest borrowing activity (8 records, 40%), consistent with the start of the academic semester.
  • 👤 All 10 members borrowed equally — every member completed exactly 2 borrowing events during the period.
  • 🎓 Students account for 70% of borrowing activity (14 of 20 records), with Faculty contributing the remaining 30%.
  • ⭐ Two books were borrowed more than once: Python Crash Course (Technology) and Atomic Habits (Self Help).
  • ⏱️ The average loan duration is 15.7 days, showing members make effective use of the standard two-week borrowing window.
  • ✔️ Return rate stands at 95% — 19 of 20 books returned on time, indicating responsible borrowing behaviour.
