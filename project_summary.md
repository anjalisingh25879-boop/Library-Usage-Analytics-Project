# Library Usage Analytics — Project Summary

**Program:** IBM SkillsBuild — Data Analytics  
**Dataset Period:** January 2026 – April 2026  

---

## Purpose

This project analyses college library borrowing patterns to identify circulation trends, popular book categories, member activity, and seasonal usage behaviour. The analysis is based on 20 borrowing records spanning January to April 2026.

---

## Dataset Snapshot

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Records | 20 |
| Unique Members | 10 (M101–M110) |
| Unique Book Titles | 18 |
| Date Range | Jan 2026 – Apr 2026 |
| Categories | Technology, Self Help, Fiction, Finance, Biography, History |
| Member Types | Student (7 members), Faculty (3 members) |

---

## Top 5 Findings

1. **Technology is the #1 category** — 5 out of 20 borrowings (25%), driven by strong interest in programming and data science titles.
2. **January had the highest borrowing activity** — 8 records (40%), consistent with the start of the academic semester.
3. **All members participated equally** — every member borrowed exactly 2 books during the period.
4. **Students account for 70% of borrowings** — 14 out of 20 records, reflecting their larger share of the membership.
5. **Average loan duration is 15.7 days** — members make efficient use of the standard two-week borrowing window.

---

## Analysis Areas Covered

- Book circulation (total, returned, active)
- Most borrowed books (*Python Crash Course*, *Atomic Habits*)
- Borrowing by category with percentage breakdown
- Member-by-member borrowing activity
- Student vs Faculty comparison
- Monthly borrowing trend (Jan–Apr 2026)
- Average days borrowed calculation
